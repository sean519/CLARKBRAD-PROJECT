Chain Reaction

  • Hold a direction to speed up. Let go and the chain crawls, so you can think.
  • Collect atoms — each one joins the front of your chain.
  • Fuse two matching numbers to double them. That is the only way to make the chain shorter.
  • Blast — every fusion fires a beam that breaks bricks. Clear them to finish the wave.
  • Three lives. Every crash costs one — including filling the chain meter, which melts the reactor down.

Hold arrows / WASD (or the pad) to accelerate · E scan · Space pause
